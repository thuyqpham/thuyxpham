Carrier—Lynx Fleet Web & Mobile, Air Design System, API Website

Senior Product Design
The Vision

Lynx Fleet is Carrier Transicold’s next-generation telematics platform — a unified, real-time intelligence hub for temperature-controlled logistics. Designed for fleet managers, drivers, and field teams, it delivers end-to-end visibility into refrigerated units across global supply chains. The platform monitors critical data: compartment temperatures, GPS location, operational status, and diagnostic alerts — all in one place, on any device.

It’s not just a dashboard — it’s a decision-making engine. By enabling proactive maintenance, regulatory compliance, and cargo integrity assurance, Lynx Fleet transforms cold chain logistics from reactive to predictive.

My Contribution

When I joined, the web MVP was built using a patchwork of disparate front-end frameworks — inconsistent, hard to maintain, and not scalable. Recognizing the long-term cost of technical debt, I championed a strategic shift: unify the UI across platforms with a cohesive, future-proof design system.

  • Ditched fragmented frameworks in favor of a single, scalable foundation
  • Built and documented a reusable Design System on top of Material UI — aligned with Carrier’s brand and product family
  • Advocated for UI cohesion across web and mobile, ensuring visual and functional consistency
  • Secured buy-in from Product & Business Teams to adopt the new system early — before it became legacy
  • Architected scalable components to support future expansion across Carrier’s entire product portfolio
The Result

A unified, clean, and intuitive user experience — reducing cognitive load and improving usability for users on the go or in the control room. The Design System became the internal standard for all Carrier digital products, accelerating future development cycles and enabling cross-platform consistency.

The platform now powers real-time decisions for global fleets — and I helped build the foundation that makes it work.

Key Achievement
  • Spearheaded early-stage refactoring of MVP to deliver scalable, maintainable architecture
  • Built and documented a reusable Design System adopted company-wide
  • Ensured visual and functional parity between web and mobile interfaces
  • Delivered a B2B SaaS platform with high data density, real-time updates, and global usability
  • Worked within Agile environments with cross-functional, large-scale teams
Why It Matters

This wasn’t just another SaaS project — it was the digital backbone for preserving the integrity of global temperature-sensitive cargo. My work helped turn a fragmented MVP into a unified, enterprise-grade platform — with lasting impact on Carrier’s product strategy and user experience.
